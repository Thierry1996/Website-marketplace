The House

A room that
remembers you.

BRASA is built around a single hearth and an unhurried clock. We dry-age our primal cuts in-house for thirty-five days, then finish them over a coal blend our pitmaster brings in from Pinar del Río.

After ten o'clock the lights drop another stop, the trio takes the corner, and the room becomes a supper club — the kind your grandfather would have recognized on Calle Ocho in 1958. We're open until the music decides to stop.
